# LEVEL ROM for Android

The phone app for measuring joint range of motion with LEVEL Inez sensors, available by email from [info@lvlmotion.com](mailto:info@lvlmotion.com).

- Live joint range of motion from sensors on the thigh, shank, chest, upper arm or forearm
- Up to 2 measured segments against an optional reference sensor
