# LEVEL Mobility for Android

The clinical phone app that turns LEVEL Inez sensor recordings into gait and mobility results (labelled LEVEL Gait on the phone). In development; available by email from [info@lvlmotion.com](mailto:info@lvlmotion.com).

- Three built-in tests: walking, Timed Up and Go, and balance
- Live feedback during walking and balance
